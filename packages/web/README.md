# @mine/web

Browser client. Vite + TypeScript + PixiJS (WebGL) for the board, plain DOM for the chrome. No framework.

## Files

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Shell: `#stage` (Pixi canvas), `#hud`, `#panel`, `#toasts`, `#modal`. |
| `src/main.ts` | Boot: load the endless world's save from IndexedDB, create `App`. |

## Controls

- Title: the left button plays the endless world (Continue with a save; New game under it replaces the world), the right one joins an Earth multiplayer session (Continue rejoins my seat; New game under it gives that seat up and joins anew). Enter presses the focused single button. Settings' New game restarts the endless world.
- Earth multiplayer (needs `npm run server`, or a build with `VITE_MINE_SERVER`): you get a random signature colour and a seat in a random session (fewer than 12 players, under 60 % of the land opened). From 70 % of the land opened a progress bar shows at the top left; at 75 % the session is complete, whoever leads wins and the standings come up (Close to look around the frozen world, Next session to join another). The map wraps east-west and opens zoomed out on the whole Earth (no fog); zoom in and click land at least 4 tiles from anyone's land to found your main base. Everyone's digging, bases and blasts show live, each player's land in their colour; your flags are yours alone. Points go straight to the score (top right; +1 per opened tile, +5 per base, −5 per wrong flag) with the session's scoreboard under it; click a player there (yourself included) to jump to their main base. A flag of yours that settles into a base takes the opened tiles around it, other players' land included (+1 per tile for you, −1 for them). A mine ends the game: your land turns back to closed tiles and the title comes back. Closing the tab keeps your seat for 10 minutes (Rejoin Earth). Zoomed out past the scale numbers are drawn at, the view is look-only.
- Drag / wheel / pinch: pan and zoom. WASD or arrows also pan. `H` returns to the main base.
- Click a base (Owned mine) to light it up and see its route; a disabled base (grey dot, struck through) also shows a bubble with its Repair button (a press anywhere else closes it and does nothing more). The main base (start cell, large diamond), the diamond button in the top-right capsule and `U` toggle the main-base panel, where it is levelled up (raising the pool cap) and the upgrades are bought; a press outside the panel closes it (and still acts).
- Points: every opened tile earns 1 and every new base 5, times the multiplier (1 + 0.1 per linked base, times the combo), into the pool (top centre). Once the pool reaches its cap nothing more opens until Cash Out moves it into credits (top right, next to the buttons).
- A new world is all fog; the first click places the main base there. Fogged tiles (dashed outline) cannot be opened or flagged; the main base sees 16 tiles, a base 9, and every opened cell 2 around it.
- Coloured tiles with a padlock are in a mining tier the mining technology (main-base panel, *Upgrades*) does not reach yet; levelling it up turns them into plain tiles. Opening one (or chording a number next to one) shows a bubble over it saying so.
- Classic mode: left-click opens, right-click cycles flag -> "?" -> clear (a "?" is only a note), clicking a number chords. Flags settle (become bases) on the next opening, never by themselves.
- Toggle mode (Settings): the flag button in the top-right capsule or `F` switches open/flag for taps.
- `Space` cashes out. `U` opens the main base. `Esc` closes.
- Touch devices (`device.ts`): tapping a number chords (as on PC), tapping a closed tile cycles flag -> "?" -> clear (the right-click cycle; a tap never opens a tile, except the first tap placing the main base), drag pans, two fingers pinch-zoom. No flag mode; Settings hides the input mode field. There is no long press on any device.
